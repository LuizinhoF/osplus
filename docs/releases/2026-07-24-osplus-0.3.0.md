## What's in this build

- A compact in-match chat that stays out of the way during play and expands
  into a stable, resizable history when opened.
- A clear recipient control: players can send to Team or All, while spectators
  can send to All, Team 1, or Team 2.
- Correct audience privacy: players only receive their own team messages plus
  All messages; spectators do not receive team-only messages.
- Reliable Enter, Escape, outside-click, and game-settings behavior, including
  suppression while the Escape menu is open.
- Connected-player presence, incoming-message sound, bottom-anchored history,
  and friendly spectator names.
